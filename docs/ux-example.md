# User-experience example (owner's words, verbatim)

Written by the owner as the reference for how Road Builder should feel to use. Every design decision for the plugin is checked
against this story. Nothing in it has been edited.

---

I want to draw a useable map for a certain chase I was witness to. We will begin by drawing the map from memory. I draw a line in vectorcraft and then it prompts me for a description. There are dozens of basic road elements in the default capacity. You draw the best match or suggest a new one.

The line represents a road oriented north south
The road consists of 1 bike lane, 1 apron, 2 south lanes, a 3 m grassy curbed margin, 2 north lanes, apron, and a bike lane.

I commit 'draw'

Based on us standards you draw as described the whole package from one end to the next along the path I have drawn. The line retains the configuration but can be modified.

I click in the middle to break the line, both lines remain connected or whatever the default is but the retain their lane configuration.

I click on the north segment and detach using the default vector craft behavior.

I draw a line connecting south segment to the north segment and prompted for some direction "connect and match, create and merge" I select create and merge and provide a description. "This is a 4 way intersection, the main route south adds a turn pocket (which you interpret as creating a third lane) and continues on with two. The west intersection is a two lane rural road, the east is a two lane rural road. There are traffic lights. There are cross walks at each side."

I commit 'draw'

You convert the new middle segment into the intersection described and now we have a road leading to an intersection and then continuing on.

I click the intersection and the panel show up describing the intersection. I clink on an element, the turn pocket and enter text "the turn pocket is probably twice as long as drawn, please adjust"

I commit 'draw'

You do the edit.

I click on the the north segment and the panel shows describing it and I click on bike lane going south and I delete it from this segment. Then draw and the drawing updates.

## Second example (owner's words, verbatim)

I click on the south segment and lengthen, add curves in vectorcraft. Each point (smooth, or angle, etc) changes the shape of the line. I add two new bezier curves and a angle point on the line.

I commit draw.

You do the edits and add curves and an abrupt 90 degree along the line.
